## If it comes out wrong

- **Too sad or gloomy:** swap "meditative" for "peaceful", or move the track to D major pentatonic. The notes stay the same, so it still crossfades fine.
- **Drums sneak in:** keep "drums" in Exclude styles and delete "wooden fish tap".
- **Sounds like a spa playlist:** delete "meditative", add "Chinese guofeng" at the front again, and exclude "new age, singing bowl".
- **Sounds Japanese:** name the guqin and xiao again and keep "Japanese, shakuhachi, koto" excluded.
- **Too many instruments:** use Alternate A.
