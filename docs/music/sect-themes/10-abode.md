## If it comes out wrong

- **A lo-fi beat appears:** keep "lo-fi hip hop beat, drums" excluded and delete "cozy".
- **Sounds like a spa or yoga track:** delete "peaceful", add "Chinese guofeng" again, keep "new age" excluded.
- **Wind chimes take over:** delete "tinkling wind chimes" from the Style. Keep them only in the Structure tags.
- **Too sleepy:** use Alternate B.
- **Sounds Japanese:** keep "Japanese, koto, shakuhachi" excluded and name the erhu and pipa again.
