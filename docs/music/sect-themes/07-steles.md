# 07 · Stele Forest of Techniques 碑林 (Skills)

Rows of carved stone tablets stand on stone turtles, with old script glowing cyan to amber. The music should feel ancient, mysterious and heavy, like walking among stones that hold old secrets. Solemn, never scary.

## If it comes out wrong

- **Sounds like a horror film:** delete "mysterious" and "dark", move "reverent and timeless" to the front, and keep "horror" excluded.
- **Monk chanting or throat singing appears:** keep "chant, throat singing" excluded. If it still leaks, delete "solemn".
- **Sounds Tibetan or like a meditation app (singing bowls):** keep "singing bowl" excluded and name the xun and guqin first.
- **Xun is missing:** Suno may not know it well. Write "xun clay ocarina" instead.
- **Too heavy for background:** use Alternate A.
