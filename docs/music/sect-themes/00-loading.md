# 00 · Loading Screen

Plays for a few seconds to half a minute while the 3D scene loads. It should feel like standing in the mist before the climb: quiet, calm, a little expectant. A bamboo flute hints at the start of the sect theme, then lets it hang.

Make this one after the gate (see "Best way to make it" below).

## Suno settings

- **Mode:** Custom (Advanced on the web)
- **Instrumental:** ON
- **Title:** Mist Before the Gate 山门前雾
- **Model:** newest available (v5 or later)
- **Weirdness:** 20%
- **Style Influence:** 70%
- **Key and tempo:** D major pentatonic (gong mode), 84 BPM with a slow half-time feel

## Style of Music

```
Chinese guofeng ambient instrumental, quiet and expectant, misty mountain dawn before a long climb, D major pentatonic (gong mode), 84 BPM with a slow half-time feel, calm floating pulse, soft xiao bamboo flute hinting a short rising pentatonic motif then resting, sparse guqin harmonics, gentle single guzheng notes, low warm drone, distant temple bell, very sparse, plenty of space between phrases, same mood start to finish, seamless loop, soft gentle background mix
```

Length: 469 characters.

## Exclude styles

```
vocals, singing, humming, choir, chant, female vocals, wordless vocals, monk chanting, drums, percussion loop, piano, synth lead, new age, singing bowl, Japanese, shakuhachi
```

## Structure (paste into the Lyrics box)

```
[Instrumental]

[Intro: low drone and one soft guqin harmonic]

[Motif: xiao plays the first rising phrase of the theme, then rests]

[Ambient: single guzheng notes, distant temple bell]

[Motif: xiao repeats the rising phrase, softer]

[Ambient: drone and guqin harmonics]

[Outro: hold a low D]

[End]
```

## Best way to make it

1. Finish the gate track first (`01-gate.md`).
2. Open your chosen gate take and pick **Cover**.
3. Paste the Style and Structure above. Set Audio Influence low to medium. You want a hint of the tune, not a copy.
4. If Cover keeps too much of the gate's energy, make it from scratch with the prompt above instead. The loading bed only needs the mood. If you make it from scratch, you can change "84 BPM with a slow half-time feel" to "60 BPM" for an even calmer pulse. Then trim it with 4.000 s bars instead of 2.857 s.

The tempo matches the gate (84 BPM) because Cover keeps the source track's tempo. The "slow half-time feel" makes it sound calmer without changing the beat, so the loop still trims on 2.857 s bars.

The site will loop this short bed, so 1 to 2 minutes is plenty.

## If it comes out wrong

- **Drums or a beat appear:** keep "drums" in Exclude styles and remove "pulse" from the Style.
- **Sounds like a spa or meditation app:** drop "ambient", add "Chinese guofeng" twice, and exclude "new age, singing bowl".
- **Sounds Japanese:** exclude "Japanese, shakuhachi, koto" and name the guqin and xiao again.
- **Too busy:** use Alternate A.
- **Long silence at the start:** trim it. The bed must make sound right away.
