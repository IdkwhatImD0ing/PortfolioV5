# 06 · Scripture Pavilion 藏经阁 (Education)

A tall pagoda library whose floors light up one by one, with scroll shelves, scrolls circling the tower and a lotus pond at its foot. The music should feel calm, studious and curious, like quietly finding something new in an old book.

## If it comes out wrong

- **A lo-fi hip hop beat appears:** "study" can pull Suno toward that. Keep "lo-fi hip hop beat, drums" excluded and delete the word "study".
- **Too sleepy:** add "curious and light" near the front and raise the liuqin.
- **Sounds Japanese:** keep "Japanese, koto, shakuhachi" excluded and name the xiao and guqin again.
- **Wood block is distracting:** delete "muyu wooden fish ticking slowly".
