# 05 · Mission Hall 任务殿 (Experience)

A grand hall with seven glowing role tablets, one per job, and a bell tower that rings when a disciple takes a new mission. The music should feel purposeful and steady, like setting out confidently on the next task.

## Suno settings

- **Mode:** Custom (Advanced on the web)
- **Instrumental:** ON
- **Title:** The Mission Bell Tolls 任务殿钟声
- **Model:** newest available (v5 or later)
- **Weirdness:** 25%
- **Style Influence:** 75%
- **Key and tempo:** A Mixolydian pentatonic (zhi mode), 100 BPM

## Style of Music

```
Chinese guofeng instrumental, purposeful steady and hopeful, setting out on a new mission, A Mixolydian pentatonic (zhi mode), 100 BPM, forward-moving even pulse, steady erhu melody, pulsing guzheng eighth-note pattern, zhongruan rhythmic strumming, light tanggu drum walking beat, deep bronze temple bell ringing at the start of each section, bright bianzhong bell chimes, dizi answering phrases, confident and focused, moderate density, same mood throughout, loop-friendly, clean warm cinematic mix, moderate loudness
```

Length: 519 characters.


## If it comes out wrong

- **Sounds like a military march:** keep "military snare, marching band" excluded and change "walking beat" to "soft hand drum".
- **The bell is missing:** Suno often skips it. Move "deep bronze temple bell" earlier in the Style, or use Alternate C.
- **Too sleepy:** raise BPM in the prompt to 104 and add "forward motion".
- **Too dramatic:** delete "cinematic" and use Alternate A.
