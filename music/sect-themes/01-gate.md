# 01 · Mountain Gate 山门 (Hero)

The first thing visitors hear. You rise out of the clouds up a long marble stair to a huge teal-roofed gate. The music should feel welcoming and grand, like arriving somewhere important. This track introduces the sect theme that returns at the summit.

**Order:** make this track first. The loading bed and the summit are built from it.

## Suno settings

- **Mode:** Custom (Advanced on the web)
- **Instrumental:** ON
- **Title:** Gate of the Ming Sect 明教山门
- **Model:** newest available (v5 or later)
- **Weirdness:** 25%
- **Style Influence:** 75%
- **Key and tempo:** D major pentatonic (gong mode), 84 BPM

## If it comes out wrong

- **No clear melody, just texture:** this one needs a tune you can hum. Use Alternate A, or move "dizi flute leads a single clear memorable theme" to the very front of the Style.
- **Sounds like a Hollywood trailer with a Chinese flute on top:** delete "cinematic" and "low strings", and raise Style Influence to 85%.
- **Sounds Japanese:** add "Chinese" once more, keep "Japanese, shakuhachi, koto" in Exclude styles, and name the erhu and pipa.
- **Intro is long and empty:** trim it later, or change the first tag to `[Intro: two bars of guzheng]`.
- **Drums too loud:** remove "tanggu drum" or change it to "very soft hand drum".
- **Big slow ending:** ignore it. You will trim the loop before the ending.
