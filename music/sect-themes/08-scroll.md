## If it comes out wrong

- **Too cute, like a cartoon or a video game shop:** keep "chiptune, kawaii" excluded and swap "playful" for "graceful".
- **Sounds Japanese:** keep "Japanese, koto" excluded and name the dizi and pipa again.
- **Clapper is annoying:** delete "light paiban wooden clapper marking the beat".
- **Too plain:** add "sparkling yangqin runs" near the front.
