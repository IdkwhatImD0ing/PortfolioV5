# 11 · Moon-Gazing Summit 望月台 (Contact)

The top of the climb at night. A crescent-moon arch frames two cranes flying toward a huge moon. This is the finale: the sect theme from the gate returns, wide and emotional, then settles. It should feel like arriving home after a long journey, hopeful and a little bittersweet.

## Suno settings

- **Mode:** Custom (Advanced on the web)
- **Instrumental:** ON
- **Title:** Gazing at the Moon 望月
- **Model:** newest available (v5 or later)
- **Weirdness:** 25%
- **Style Influence:** 80%
- **Key and tempo:** D major pentatonic (gong mode), 84 BPM (same as the gate, with a broader half-time feel)

## Best way to make it

The summit must reuse the gate's tune, so do not generate it from scratch first.

1. Finish the gate track (`01-gate.md`) and pick your favourite take.
2. Open that take's menu and choose **Cover**.
3. Paste the Style, Exclude styles and Structure below.
4. Set Audio Influence medium to high. Higher keeps more of the gate's melody.
5. If the theme drifts, raise Audio Influence and try again. If it sounds too much like the gate, lower it.
6. If a take starts well but ends early, use **Extend** from a good spot instead of starting over.

The same tempo (84 BPM) as the gate helps Cover keep the tune. The "half-time feel" makes it sound slower and wider without changing the tempo.

## If it comes out wrong

- **The theme does not match the gate:** you generated from scratch. Use Cover on the gate take, with Audio Influence higher.
- **Too much Western orchestra:** delete "cinematic" and "low strings", and raise Style Influence to 90%.
- **Too sad:** swap "bittersweet" for "peaceful".
- **Big crash ending or long fade:** ignore it and trim the loop before the ending.
- **Drums too loud for a finale bed:** delete "dagu drum swells" or use Alternate A.
- **Suona too shrill in Alternate B:** delete "distant suona call".
