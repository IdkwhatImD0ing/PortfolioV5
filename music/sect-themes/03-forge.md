# 03 · Artifact Forge Peak 炼器峰 (Projects)

A two-tier forge hall around a colossal bronze cauldron glowing molten orange, with sparks, anvils and chimneys. Eleven prize-winning artifacts orbit the cauldron in a slow ring. The music should feel proud and rhythmic, like skilled hands hammering hot bronze in a steady work rhythm.

## Suno settings

- **Mode:** Custom (Advanced on the web)
- **Instrumental:** ON
- **Title:** Fire of the Artifact Forge 炼器之火
- **Model:** newest available (v5 or later)
- **Weirdness:** 30%
- **Style Influence:** 75%
- **Key and tempo:** E Dorian pentatonic (shang mode), 108 BPM

## Style of Music

```
Chinese guofeng instrumental, cinematic xianxia forge, proud driving and warm, E Dorian pentatonic (shang mode), 108 BPM, steady work rhythm, yangqin hammered dulcimer ostinato, fast pipa picking, tanggu and paigu drums in a steady pattern, metallic hammer-strike percussion on the beat, deep sheng chords swelling like bellows, bold erhu melody on top, dizi flute doubling it an octave higher, bright small cymbal accents like sparks, molten bronze glow, full and balanced, even energy throughout, loop-friendly, punchy clear mix, moderate loudness

```

## If it comes out wrong

- **Turns into rock or metal:** keep "electric guitar, rock drums, heavy metal" excluded and delete "driving" from the Style.
- **Sounds like Japanese taiko:** keep "taiko, Japanese" excluded and name "tanggu" and "paigu" again.
- **Drum solo in the middle:** change the Interlude tag to `[Interlude: yangqin and erhu, drums steady underneath]`.
- **Too loud and busy for background:** use Alternate B.
- **No anvil sound:** Suno rarely makes real sound effects. "Metallic percussion" is the closest it gets. That is fine.
